Ghi chú phát hành

Trang này được hiển thị bằng tiếng Anh.

Iltonggi is not released yet. This page lists, in plain words, what changed in each development build we send to TestFlight. The newest build is at the top.

1.0.0+26

2026-10-29 10:55 KST

  • On the start screen, the three characters of the title land one by one with a thud, like stamps.
  • Each time a character lands, the screen shakes a little and dust flies up, with a drum beat and a vibration.
  • The last character hits the hardest.
  • Tap Skip to go straight to the start menu without waiting.

1.0.0+25

2026-10-29 10:13 KST

  • You will be able to dress the map in a new look. Three art-style map themes are coming soon (not available yet).
  • Preview them in Settings → Map theme. The current map stays free, always.
  • Each theme softens the borders as if painted with a brush, and the fog and night scenes follow the same style.

1.0.0+24

2026-10-29 KST

  • You can now drag the map all the way to its edges. Tap a town hidden behind a button and the map slides it into view.
  • Map colors are calmer and easier on the eyes.
  • The fog is now see-through, so you can faintly make out lands you have not seen yet.
  • A new slot has appeared in the market, marked as coming soon.

1.0.0+23

2026-10-29 08:25 KST

  • A diplomacy screen has arrived. You can send tribute to a neighboring realm, or ask for an alliance or a joint attack. An alliance takes about two tributes to win.
  • On the household screen you can grow your towns and gather wealth.
  • The spy screen now has scouting suggestions that tell you where to look.
  • Added text for all 11 languages.

1.0.0+22

2026-10-29 06:12 KST

  • Polished 196 translations across the 11 languages.
  • Fixed the difficulty selector layout in Russian when large text is on.

1.0.0+21

2026-10-29 06:09 KST

  • Added the privacy notice.
  • Improved layouts so text is not cut off with large text sizes.
  • Made buttons and screens easier to follow with VoiceOver.

1.0.0+20

2026-10-29 KST

  • The app is lighter.
  • A new screen lets you decorate portrait frames, seals, and your realm's banner.

1.0.0+19

2026-10-29 KST

  • The market screen has arrived. You can try a famed general for one game, and there is a restore-purchases button.
  • Items are still coming soon.

1.0.0+18

2026-10-29 KST

  • General portraits now appear across the screens.
  • Snow settles on the map in winter.
  • Added support for Low Power Mode to save battery.

1.0.0+17

2026-10-28 KST

  • Real-world weather now affects battles. You can choose how much in Settings.
  • You now have 3 manual save slots.
  • You can choose Easy, Normal, or Hard difficulty.

1.0.0+16

2026-10-28 22:32 KST

  • Petals and falling leaves drift across the map with the seasons.
  • Battle screens now show the sky and the soldiers.

1.0.0+15

2026-10-28 21:24 KST

  • Added a guided first-time quest and a rules screen.
  • Your first game is a short 20 turns, and the first two rounds are safe.

1.0.0+14

2026-10-28 KST

  • Act 1 now opens with a story written in ink.
  • You can send spies to peek at other towns.

1.0.0+13

2026-10-28 KST

  • You can now skip the intro.
  • It is much easier to see which town you picked and where you can go.

1.0.0+12

2026-10-28 KST

  • The map now changes through five moods from dawn to night, following your local time.

1.0.0+11

2026-10-28 18:44 KST

  • The first-launch intro is now a new cinematic of about 11 seconds.

1.0.0+10

2026-10-28 KST

  • The game now supports 11 languages. You can change the language in Settings.

1.0.0+9

2026-10-28 KST

  • After the opening, a start menu with lacquered buttons appears.

1.0.0+8

2026-10-28 KST

  • Launching the app now plays an opening where ink spreads, four realms' banners unfurl, and a seal is stamped.

1.0.0+7

2026-10-28 KST

  • The map is now a 3D-looking quarter view.
  • Zoom in to see thatched villages and stone fortresses.

1.0.0+6

2026-10-28 08:21 KST

  • The home-screen icon is now a red seal reading 統.

1.0.0+5

2026-10-28 08:14 KST

  • You can create and keep your own general cards, and bring one into a game as your king.

1.0.0+4

2026-10-28 KST

  • Added a troop-selection window before attacks and battle result animations.
  • You can also turn on auto-battle.

1.0.0+3

2026-10-28 KST

  • You can cancel when you pick the wrong town.
  • The game now installs on more iPhones.

1.0.0+2

2026-10-28 01:21 KST

  • For the first time, you can play a full game on iPhone.
  • Pick a realm and difficulty, then tap towns to grow and attack.
