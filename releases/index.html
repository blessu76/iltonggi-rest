Release notes

Iltonggi is not released yet. This page lists, in plain words, what changed in each development build we send to TestFlight. The newest build is at the top.

1.0.0+45

2026-10-30 13:35 KST

  • You can now choose an Era when starting a new game. Heroes keeps famous kings and generals from every age together in one game (no years shown); Historical starts in 391 with the real kings and capitals of that year.
  • In Historical, Gwanggaeto, Jinsa, Naemul Maripgan and Isipum begin at Gungnae, Hanseong, Seorabeol and Geumgwan Gaya, and kings change in their true order as the years pass — Asin in 392, Jangsu in 412 and onward.
  • Historical generals belong to their own age. Where no name survives, a commander is shown as “General of …”, and real generals such as Jinmu, Bak Jesang, Isabu and Kim Yusin step forward in the years the records name them.
  • Events such as the move of the capital to Pyeongyang in 427 arrive as yearly news. There is also a new act opening for Historical and short biographies for the people of 391.

1.0.0+44

2026-10-30 12:51 KST

  • The game now fills the iPad screen. Windows, buttons and text scale with the screen (about 1.5× on a 13-inch iPad), so it feels like the phone version. Phone screens are unchanged.
  • On iPad, the black bars above and below the intro, the title menu and the act cinematics are much thinner, and the title, seal and menu buttons grow to fit the screen.
  • On iPad, the Diplomacy, Economy, Spy and Save windows are taller so more fits at once, and the one-page rules have roomier lines that are easier to read.
  • You can zoom further into the map on iPad, and on a 13-inch iPad the general portraits in the New Game screen are one size larger.

1.0.0+43

2026-10-30 12:23 KST

  • Troops no longer grow on their own each turn. Every region, capitals included, gains troops only by recruiting. Each levy is bigger the more people a region has, the higher its morale and the more you have developed it — large castles hold more people, so they recruit best.
  • When you pick a region, its population (now/max) shows on the top line of the region card. Recruiting spends population, which refills a little every turn.
  • Rival kingdoms recruit by the same rule. On higher difficulties they raise more troops per levy. The Baekje and Ming traits are now "Recruiting +50%" and "Recruiting +20%".
  • Choosing a language in Settings now shows it on the row right away, and the language list sits in two columns so you can see it all at once. Roads are clear on all three paid map styles, and save slots show a To the End game as "To the End · Turn N".

1.0.0+42

2026-10-30 11:52 KST

  • When you set up a new game, a "勝 Victory" scroll now lets you choose how it ends: the usual game where the kingdom with the most land wins after a set number of turns (30 on Easy, 40 on Normal and Hard), or "To the End" — no turn limit, and you win only by capturing every rival king. Your choice is remembered for the next game.
  • In a To the End game, the remaining kings show as gold pips in the status card and rival capitals get a vermilion ring. When a king is captured, the seal cracks and a 捕 stamp comes down. Rival capitals hidden in fog stay hidden until scouts or spies reveal them.
  • Kings and generals can now move to any castle connected through your own land, however far (1 action · troops stay put). Tap "Move King" or "Move General" under the region card, then pick a spot with a gold ring. Rival kingdoms follow the same rule, sending their kings to safety and their generals to the front.
  • History fixes: Isabu became Minister of War in 541, and Sadaham's biography now tells what the records say about the land he was given. The base Goguryeo general Ondal is now Moduru and the Gaya general Kim Muryeok is now Inoe, so they no longer share names with the Famous Generals pack.

1.0.0+41

2026-10-30 06:56 KST

  • Troops can now move up to 3 regions at once. One region costs 1 action; 2–3 regions through your own land cost 2. With only one action left, long moves are blocked and you're told why. Rival kingdoms follow the same rule.
  • When you pick a unit, the map tells its reach apart: attacks get a red seal ring, moves a paper-white dashed ring, and 2–3 region moves two vermilion pips. The "Move" and "Attack" chips in the region card show just one of them.
  • When sending troops, [25%] [50%] [75%] [All] buttons now sit above the slider. The default is still all of them.
  • The cards on the map have a new look: black lacquer plaques with gold double lines, the chosen region lifts up on hanji paper, and the actions left this turn show as 行 seal pips above the End Turn button. Roads are easier to see, too.

1.0.0+40

2026-10-30 06:17 KST

  • You can now command in person when the enemy attacks your towns too. Tap "Defend in person" before the battle and answer with hold, charge or flank over three rounds.
  • Towns with walls fight from behind their walls, small towns from behind a palisade. Enemies before your walls often charge, so read the scouts' report.
  • If you have a neighbouring town, "Abandon" lets you fall back and save some of your troops.
  • When several attacks come in one turn, you're asked one at a time. "Leave all this turn" hands the rest to your officers at once.

1.0.0+39

2026-10-30 05:45 KST

  • Every remaining app-style screen is now a game screen. The Market, Style, Map themes, Hero card box, Card maker, Famous generals, Side-story people, Rules, Past tales and New game all open on a hanji panel with a brush-stroke title and lacquer buttons.
  • Full screens go back with ◀ in the top left, and the main button sits in the bottom right. Every tap target is at least finger-sized.
  • Confirmations such as deleting a card, expanding the drawer or the large map preview use the same hanji window, and delete now has its own danger button.
  • On the New game screen, game tiles wrap onto two rows when space is tight, so their names don't shrink.

1.0.0+38

2026-10-30 05:35 KST

  • In the intro, each time the map lands with a boom, that act's name (Act 1 Three Kingdoms to Act 11 Russia) now slams onto the middle of the screen like a seal. The small caption in the lower left is gone.
  • Settings have been rebuilt into five tabs: Display, Sound, Game, Map & Style and Info. Sound effects volume and vibration can now be set on their own, and you can pick the difficulty for your next new game there too.
  • To make map themes easy to find, the start menu has a Change map button, and you can also change the map from the pause menu and from Settings › Map & Style.
  • Dev News now opens inside the game instead of sending you out to a browser. Find it under Settings › Info and in the pause menu.
  • The Diplomacy, Economy and Spy windows, save slots and the difficulty picker now use the same hanji windows and lacquer buttons. Economy button notes no longer break in the middle of a word, and a soft shadow shows when a list has more below.

1.0.0+37

2026-10-29 23:35 KST

  • The windows you see most during a game have been redrawn. The pre-battle prompt, auto battle, direct command, the end-of-game result, the pause menu and spy reports now sit on hanji paper with an ink border, a thin gold line and a brush-stroke title band.
  • Buttons now use the same lacquer style as the start menu. They share one size, the main button always sits at the bottom right of a window, and Close sits at the right end of the title.
  • When you win, a sweep of gold light passes over the result window. Sharing your result image now shows the preview inside the result window instead of opening a second one.
  • Short notices, like confirming a save, now appear on an ink strip.

1.0.0+36

2026-10-29 23:07 KST

  • Short story scenes, about 30 seconds each, now play between acts. One plays the first time you open an act, and you can skip it with "Skip" in the top right.
  • The prologue "The Blank Book" shows four kingdoms rising from a single drop of ink, the interlude "Six Hundred Years of Sleep" follows warriors raising their banners across the sea, and the interlude "The Red River" sets the banners of the Yuan, the Unified Kingdom and the Ming face to face.
  • Watch them again from the book chip on the start menu or "Past Tales" in Settings. A locked tale opens once you win the act before it.
  • If you set "Act intro story" to off in Settings, these scenes won't play either.

1.0.0+35

2026-10-29 19:22 KST

  • Act 3 of the continental story, "Continent of Red Turbans", is here. Win Act 2 once and an Act 3 tile appears when you start a new game.
  • In the mid-14th century, the Red Turbans spill across the border while the Yuan and the Ming fight over the continent. With the Unified Kingdom, three powers face off. Plant your banner over Yingtian across the great river, or hold the most towns when the game ends, to win.
  • A new map: cross the Yalu to Liaoyang, cross the western sea to Dengzhou, or sail the southern sea to Qingyuan.
  • Read the stories of ten people of the era, including Zhu Yuanzhang, Xu Da, Toghon Temür and Jeong Seun, on the new-game screen.
  • Every nation in Acts 2 and 3 now has its own colors and soldier gear: horned helmets for the shogunate's warriors, fur-trimmed helmets for the Yuan, and red head wraps for the Ming.

1.0.0+34

2026-10-29 18:27 KST

  • Act 2 of the continental story, "Wind Across the Sea", is here. Win Act 1 once and an Act 2 tile appears when you start a new game.
  • In the late 13th century, the Unified Kingdom crosses the sea to the islands, where the Kyushu Warriors and the Kamakura Shogunate stand together. Plant your banner over Kamakura, or hold the most towns when the game ends, to win.
  • A new map: sail from Happo past Iki to Hakata, or cross the eastern sea to Izumo.
  • Read the stories of nine people of the era, including Kim Banggyeong, Hōjō Tokimune and Takezaki Suenaga, on the new-game screen. Winning the act brings its closing scene.
  • The new messages are available in all 11 languages.

1.0.0+33

2026-10-29 17:44 KST

  • "Command in person" is now open on the pre-battle screen. Tap it to fight three rounds yourself, choosing Hold, Charge or Flank each round. Auto Battle is still the default.
  • Hold beats Charge, Charge beats Flank, and Flank beats Hold. Scouts hint at the enemy's next move, and they are more reliable when a general leads you. Enemies behind walls often hold formation.
  • Generals, troop numbers and weather count exactly as in Auto Battle. Reading the enemy well gives a small edge, but it won't turn a badly lost fight around. If it goes wrong, retreat to bring half your troops home.
  • The new messages are available in all 11 languages.

2026-10-29 16:52 KST

  • On Easy, every kingdom is now about as winnable as the others. Hard picks like Gaya and Baekje are noticeably easier; Normal and Hard are unchanged.
  • The new messages are available in all 11 languages.
