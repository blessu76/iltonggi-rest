Iltonggi is not released yet. This page lists, in plain words, what changed in each development build we send to TestFlight. The newest build is at the top.

1.0.0+23 uploading

2026-10-29 KST

  • A diplomacy screen has arrived. You can send tribute to a neighboring realm, or ask for an alliance or a joint attack. An alliance takes about two tributes to win.
  • On the household screen you can grow your towns and gather wealth.
  • The spy screen now has scouting suggestions that tell you where to look.
  • Added text for all 11 languages.

1.0.0+22

2026-10-29 06:12 KST

  • Polished 196 translations across the 11 languages.
  • Fixed the difficulty selector layout in Russian when large text is on.

1.0.0+21

2026-10-29 06:09 KST

  • Added the privacy notice.
  • Improved layouts so text is not cut off with large text sizes.
  • Made buttons and screens easier to follow with VoiceOver.
