When a game screen looks cheap, the usual reaction is to add more: more gold, more borders, more pictures. When we looked again at the Iltonggi screens, the problem was the opposite. We already had enough materials. The trouble was that we decorated everything, and the decorations argued with each other. This post records that diagnosis and the direction we chose. Much of the work is still ahead.

## Every box wore the same frame

The top band of the board, the buttons, the action row and the dialog boxes all used the same double gold border with corner ornaments. Nothing stood out as more important than anything else. Only the red end-of-turn marker signaled that something mattered more. Decoration needs a rank, and we had none.

## Four materials in one place

Black lacquer with gold trim, a hanji window with an ink brush stripe, a flat white card with an underlined link, and outlined boxes all appeared together. When one screen switches materials several times, a player cannot tell which parts belong together.

## Too many accent colors

Five or six accent colors competed for attention: saturated kingdom chips, a gold king tile, purple labels, a red turn marker, and green and red range cells. The color definitions were also scattered across several places, so a change in one spot did not reach the others.

## The map was the noisiest part

The map labels came in four styles, and the map screen used almost none of the shared design tokens. In portrait view, the map also took less of the screen than it should. The exact share is still uncertain, because our estimate came from looking at screenshots rather than measuring. We will measure it properly in a later step.

## The direction: ink, hanji and one gold line

Materials are limited to black lacquer and hanji. Gold is used only as a line. Vermilion appears once per screen. Decoration is split into three ranks, and only the first rank gets the full treatment.

## What is done and what is not

The work is split into five steps. So far we have reworked the top band of the board and the map name tags. The other screens have not changed yet. The battle screen, the ledger and the card screen have not been confirmed on recent device captures, so we will check those diagnoses again before changing them.
