Support

If you run into a problem or have a suggestion while playing, please write to us by email.

Contact · ipe.haven@gmail.com
We aim to reply within a few business days.

Helpful details to include

  • Your device model and iOS version
  • App version (Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Iltonggi)
  • What was happening when the problem occurred, with a screenshot if you have one

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. It may be lost if you delete the App or switch devices.

Is there an Android version?

We are releasing on iPhone first. We will share news about other platforms later.

Do you collect personal information?

The app does not. The website collects basic visit statistics through Google Analytics. See the Privacy Policy for details.
