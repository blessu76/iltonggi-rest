From Three Kingdoms to a Wider Map, Where Iltonggi Stands Now

2026-11-08 12:00 KST

Iltonggi began as a small land grab on the Korean peninsula in the age of the Three Kingdoms. Over the last few weeks the map has grown a great deal. This post is a plain status note on where things stand: what the acts are, how they connect, and what is still unfinished. Everything here is a snapshot of development, and any of it may change.

1. Act 1 is still the starting point

Act 1 is the Three Kingdoms: Goguryeo, Baekje, Silla and Gaya, on a map of 24 regions. It is the act where the rules are taught, and it is where the first short game takes place. We have tried to keep it as the quiet place to begin, however wide the rest becomes.

2. Acts 2 and 3 move the clock

Act 2 starts in the year 1274 on a map of 21 regions, and Act 3 starts in 1359 on a map of 24 regions. From Act 2 onward the kingdom you play is the United Kingdom, a kingdom of our own invention that rises in a different land each time. We will say more in later posts about why we chose this, and what we mark as adaptation when we do it.

3. The map reaches the sea and the wider world

The newer acts leave the peninsula entirely. Act 4 is "Treasure Fleet of the Southern Seas", and its map grew from 25 to 38 regions. Act 5 is "Stargazer of the Silk Road", and Act 6 is "The Sea of the Renaissance". Further on there are Act 8, "City on the Lake", Act 9, "The Road Above the Clouds", and Act 10, "The Oldest Land". Each act has its own goal, its own set of nations and its own pace, so it should not feel like the same game repainted.

Acts open as you win the ones before them. You do not have to play them all, and you do not have to play them in a hurry.

4. A world map that remembers

If you zoom the map out all the way, and then once more, a world map opens. The lands of the acts you have unified are painted in the same color there. It is a small thing, but it makes a long run feel like a single journey rather than a list of separate games.

5. Smaller changes that matter

Some of the recent changes are not about new lands at all. If the file that lets you resume a game ever gets corrupted, the app now recovers using the game before it. There is also a "Report a problem" item in Settings that lets you email an error log, so that when something goes wrong you can tell us without describing it from memory. Several maps also gained more regions, such as the start screen of Act 2 and the western lands of Act 3.

Finally

There are more places we would like to draw, and some of them are still only plans on paper. We would rather tell you that plainly than promise a list. As each act becomes ready we will write about the choices behind it, including the ones we decided not to make.
