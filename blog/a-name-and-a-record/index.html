A Name and a Record, Nicknames and the Scoreboard

2026-11-13 12:00 KST

A land grab is more fun when you can look back at what you did. Iltonggi is getting a nickname and a records screen, and it is being prepared to work with Game Center. This post explains how they work. It is a snapshot of development, and parts of it are still being prepared and may change.

1. A nickname, asked once

After you finish your first game, the tutorial game included, and come back to the menu, the app asks for a nickname one time. A nickname is 2 to 12 characters. You can skip it with "Later", and the app will not ask again by itself. You change it later from the top of the records screen.

The nickname is stored on your device only. It is the name you are called inside the app, and it is not synced to other devices.

2. The records screen

A trophy chip on the top row of the menu opens the records screen. It shows your nickname with a button to change it, and then your own records:

  • victories
  • unifications
  • fastest Act 1 unification on Hard, counted in turns
  • best win streak
  • games played

This screen works the same way on every device, whether or not Game Center is available. The tutorial game does not count toward any of it.

3. Four scoreboards

For Game Center we planned four boards: victories, unifications, the fastest Act 1 unification on Hard, and the best win streak. Three are the higher the better. The fastest unification is the other way round, where fewer turns rank higher.

We tied the fastest-unification board to Act 1 and Hard on purpose. The maps differ from act to act, and Easy, Normal and Hard are not the same difficulty, so turn counts from different acts or settings could not be compared fairly. If it later seems worth having a board for each difficulty, we will think about it again.

4. Whose name appears on the board

This is the one thing worth knowing in advance. The board will show your Game Center name, not your in-app nickname. The app says so on the records screen, so that nobody is surprised to see a different name there.

Sending scores happens quietly. If you are not signed in, or if a score fails to send, the app does not interrupt you, and it tries again later. You can play normally without ever signing in.

5. What is still pending

The Game Center connection itself is still being prepared, and we are not saying here that it is on. Your nickname, your records and the records screen do not depend on it. We will say plainly when the connection is ready, not before.

Finally

Records from before this feature arrived do not count backwards, so everyone starts from zero together. A name and a number are small things, but they are the traces that show a journey was yours.
