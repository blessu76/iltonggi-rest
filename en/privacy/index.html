Privacy Policy

Effective: November 1, 2026 · Operated by Davy Kim (ipe Studio)

In short: The Iltonggi app collects no personal information. This website collects basic visit statistics through Google Analytics (section 3).

1. Information we collect — none

Iltonggi ("the App") is a mobile game operated by an individual developer, Davy Kim (ipe Studio). The App has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not collect or send to any server any personal information such as your name, email, contact details, location or device identifiers. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the App.

The App contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. Weather information

To show regional weather, the App downloads a public file (weather.json) from iltonggi.ipe.rest whenever it is online. The file collects the weather for the 24 towns in the game once an hour and is the same for every user. The App sends no personal information in doing so — no location, device identifier or game data — and it does not contact any weather provider directly.

As with any ordinary web request, the server log of our host (GitHub Pages) may record your device's IP address; we do not collect or use that log. If the weather cannot be downloaded (for example, offline), the App shows clear weather and the game works normally. The last weather received is stored temporarily on your device and is deleted when you delete the App.

Weather data: MET Norway (CC BY 4.0).

3. The website and visit statistics (Google Analytics)

This section applies only to the iltonggi.ipe.rest website, not to the App. The App collects nothing and contains no analytics (see section 1); website pages shown inside the App do not load Google Analytics.

On the website we use Google Analytics 4 to see which pages are read. It records page views, the page you came from, your browser and device type, an approximate location (country or city) and clicks on links that leave our site (the link address only). It sets cookies in your browser (such as _ga, which can last up to two years) to tell visits apart. We do not ask for your name or email in analytics, and we cannot identify you from it. Google processes this data on our behalf, which can include servers outside Korea.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so ordinary web request information (such as your IP address) may be sent to Google. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub's server-log policies apply.

To opt out, block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's Analytics opt-out add-on (tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). The website works the same without them.

4. Third parties

The App collects no personal information, so it has nothing to share with or sell to third parties. On the website, Google (visit statistics) and GitHub (hosting) process data as described in section 3; we do not sell it. The App is distributed through the Apple App Store; information related to downloads is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Weather data is provided by MET Norway. We fetch it ourselves and publish it as a public file, so no information about App users is passed to MET Norway.

5. Questions and your rights

To request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal information, or to ask any question, write to ipe.haven@gmail.com. The App holds no personal information, and website analytics data is not tied to your name or email, so there may be nothing we can find to act on.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 14 (or the minimum age in your region), and it collects no personal information from any user.

9. Changes

If the App later adds accounts, payments or online features that change what is collected, we will update this policy first and change the effective date.
