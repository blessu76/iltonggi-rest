Iltonggi · Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect — none

Iltonggi ("the App") is a mobile game operated by an individual developer, Davy Kim (ipe Studio). The App has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not collect or send to any server any personal information such as your name, email, contact details, location or device identifiers. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the App. Any nickname you choose in the game and your game records are stored only on your device and are not sent to or collected by us.

The App contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. Weather information

To show regional weather, the App downloads a public file (weather.json) from iltonggi.ipe.rest whenever it is online. The file collects the weather for the 24 towns in the game once an hour and is the same for every user. The App sends no personal information in doing so — no location, device identifier or game data — and it does not contact any weather provider directly.

As with any ordinary web request, the server log of our host (GitHub Pages) may record your device's IP address; we do not collect or use that log. If the weather cannot be downloaded (for example, offline), the App shows clear weather and the game works normally. The last weather received is stored temporarily on your device and is deleted when you delete the App.

Weather data: MET Norway (CC BY 4.0).

Weather data is provided by MET Norway. We fetch it ourselves and publish it as a public file, so no information about App users is passed to MET Norway.

Iltonggi · Terms of Use

3. Nature of the service

The App's core features work offline; it uses the internet only, optionally, to show the weather (if it cannot be downloaded, clear weather is shown). Game data is stored on your device. Data may be lost if you change devices, delete the App or reset your device, and we may not be able to restore it.

4. Weather information

The weather shown in the App is reference information used for the game's presentation and rules (battle modifiers). We do not guarantee that it matches actual weather conditions or that it will always be available. If weather information is missing or out of date, clear weather may be used.

Weather data: MET Norway (CC BY 4.0).
