Iltonggi is not released yet. This page lists, in plain words, what changed in each development build we send to TestFlight. The newest build is at the top.

1.0.0+36

2026-10-29 23:07 KST

  • Short story scenes, about 30 seconds each, now play between acts. One plays the first time you open an act, and you can skip it with "Skip" in the top right.
  • The prologue "The Blank Book" shows four kingdoms rising from a single drop of ink, the interlude "Six Hundred Years of Sleep" follows warriors raising their banners across the sea, and the interlude "The Red River" sets the banners of the Yuan, the Unified Kingdom and the Ming face to face.
  • Watch them again from the book chip on the start menu or "Past Tales" in Settings. A locked tale opens once you win the act before it.
  • If you set "Act intro story" to off in Settings, these scenes won't play either.

1.0.0+35

2026-10-29 19:22 KST

  • Act 3 of the continental story, "Continent of Red Turbans", is here. Win Act 2 once and an Act 3 tile appears when you start a new game.
  • In the mid-14th century, the Red Turbans spill across the border while the Yuan and the Ming fight over the continent. With the Unified Kingdom, three powers face off. Plant your banner over Yingtian across the great river, or hold the most towns when the game ends, to win.
  • A new map: cross the Yalu to Liaoyang, cross the western sea to Dengzhou, or sail the southern sea to Qingyuan.
  • Read the stories of ten people of the era, including Zhu Yuanzhang, Xu Da, Toghon Temür and Jeong Seun, on the new-game screen.
  • Every nation in Acts 2 and 3 now has its own colors and soldier gear: horned helmets for the shogunate's warriors, fur-trimmed helmets for the Yuan, and red head wraps for the Ming.

1.0.0+34

2026-10-29 18:27 KST

  • Act 2 of the continental story, "Wind Across the Sea", is here. Win Act 1 once and an Act 2 tile appears when you start a new game.
  • In the late 13th century, the Unified Kingdom crosses the sea to the islands, where the Kyushu Warriors and the Kamakura Shogunate stand together. Plant your banner over Kamakura, or hold the most towns when the game ends, to win.
  • A new map: sail from Happo past Iki to Hakata, or cross the eastern sea to Izumo.
  • Read the stories of nine people of the era, including Kim Banggyeong, Hōjō Tokimune and Takezaki Suenaga, on the new-game screen. Winning the act brings its closing scene.
  • The new messages are available in all 11 languages.

1.0.0+33

2026-10-29 17:44 KST

  • "Command in person" is now open on the pre-battle screen. Tap it to fight three rounds yourself, choosing Hold, Charge or Flank each round. Auto Battle is still the default.
  • Hold beats Charge, Charge beats Flank, and Flank beats Hold. Scouts hint at the enemy's next move, and they are more reliable when a general leads you. Enemies behind walls often hold formation.
  • Generals, troop numbers and weather count exactly as in Auto Battle. Reading the enemy well gives a small edge, but it won't turn a badly lost fight around. If it goes wrong, retreat to bring half your troops home.
  • The new messages are available in all 11 languages.

1.0.0+32

2026-10-29 16:52 KST

  • After two or more losses in a row, the defeat screen now shows one battle tip. It is picked from what happened in that game — a fallen capital, a weaker army, fighting several kingdoms with no ally, and so on.
  • On Easy, every kingdom is now about as winnable as the others. Hard picks like Gaya and Baekje are noticeably easier; Normal and Hard are unchanged.
  • The new messages are available in all 11 languages.

1.0.0+31

2026-10-29 16:20 KST

  • The people of the upcoming game set in 551 now match their era. Generals from other centuries are gone, and posts whose holders are not recorded are shown as "General of …". The Gaya king appears as "King of Daegaya" instead of a name.
  • When you pick the 551 game in New Game, each kingdom's people are listed; tap one to read a short biography. Where records disagree, we say so.
  • The other kingdoms in the 551 game now go after the three Han River towns and guard the ones they hold first.
  • The new messages are available in all 11 languages.

1.0.0+30

2026-10-29 15:47 KST

  • You can now share your game result, your general's card, or a famous general's portrait as an image. Tap "Share" in the results window, the card view, or the biography. The image is made only on your device.
  • The new game set in 551 now has its own victory condition: capture Hanseong, Michuhol and Gugwonseong and hold all three for 3 rounds in a row. Goguryeo, which starts with them, wins by keeping them to the end. Progress shows in the news bar.
  • We added the groundwork for continuing your game on another device (switched off for now).
  • The new messages are available in all 11 languages.

1.0.0+29

2026-10-29 15:00 KST

  • On small screens (like iPhone SE), the buttons at the bottom of the results window could be hidden. They now always stay at the bottom, and only the content above scrolls.
  • We're preparing a new game on the same map with the same four kingdoms but a different starting layout: the year 551, with Baekje and Silla starting as allies and eyeing the north.
  • At the top of the new-game screen you can now choose between the original game and the new one. The new game opens only in test builds for now.
  • The new game's opening story and messages are available in all 11 languages.

1.0.0+28

2026-10-29 15:00 KST

  • From your third win, the results screen shows a small one-line card about decorations. Nothing appears after your first or second win.
  • On that card, "Take a look" and "Later" are the same size. Tap "Don't show again" and it won't come back.
  • Suggestions appear at most once a day in total, and the same one never repeats within a week. Turn them all off with "Turn off suggestions" in Settings.
  • The new messages from the last update are now checked in all 11 languages.

1.0.0+27

2026-10-29 13:44 KST

  • You can now view a locked map theme at a larger size with "Big view". If you like it, you can try it for just one next game. No purchase needed.
  • When your drawer is full, two equally sized buttons let you choose: delete a card or add one more slot.
  • When you come back after a few days, we let you know your game is still there and give you a small gift.
  • If you leave to the menu mid-game, the current game is saved and you'll see "Saved."
  • In Settings, turn on "Hide suggestions" to stop recommendation prompts. We also added a "Ask a grown-up to buy" notice for kids' devices at the bottom of the shop.

1.0.0+26

2026-10-29 10:55 KST

  • On the start screen, the three characters of the title land one by one with a thud, like stamps.
  • Each time a character lands, the screen shakes a little and dust flies up, with a drum beat and a vibration.
  • The last character hits the hardest.
  • Tap Skip to go straight to the start menu without waiting.

  • Each theme softens the borders as if painted with a brush, and the fog and night scenes follow the same style.
